As the clock winds down on 2016, there’s a growing emphasis for 2017 and the years to follow. And that emphasis is on the importance for knowledge workers to be able to focus in order to do their best work. The tool sets and work environments of the modern “office worker” are maximized for distraction, not for in-depth output.
